# Bleeding control steps

1. Put on gloves if you have them. A plastic bag over your hand also works.
2. Remove or cut away clothing so you can see the wound.
3. Do not remove objects stuck in the wound. Press around them instead.
4. Place a clean cloth, gauze, or shirt directly on the wound.
5. Press hard with the heel of your hand or both hands. Firm pressure is what stops bleeding.
6. Ask the app to start a timer named "pressure" for ten minutes.
7. Keep pressing until the timer ends. Do not lift the cloth to look.
8. If blood soaks through, add more cloth on top. Never remove the first layer.
9. If the wound is on an arm or leg, raise it above the level of the heart while you keep pressing.
10. When the timer ends, check if bleeding has slowed to a trickle or stopped.
11. If it has stopped, wrap a bandage, cloth strip, or elastic wrap firmly over the dressing to make a pressure dressing.
12. Wrap snugly but check that fingers or toes stay warm and pink. Loosen slightly if they turn cold or blue.
13. If bleeding continues, apply pressure for another ten minutes. Restart the timer.
14. For bleeding from an arm or leg that is spurting, pooling, or still heavy after firm pressure, tell the app the bleeding is not stopping. It will guide you through a tourniquet.
15. Keep the person warm and lying down. Raise the legs if there is no injury there.
16. Watch for shock: pale or gray skin, sweating, confusion, fast breathing. Shock means call for help now.
17. Once bleeding is controlled, rinse small wounds with clean water and cover with a clean dressing.
18. Any wound that is deep, gaping, from a bite, or very dirty needs medical care within hours.
