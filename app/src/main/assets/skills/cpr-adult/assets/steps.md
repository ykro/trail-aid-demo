# Adult CPR steps

1. Check that the area is safe for you and the person.
2. Tap the shoulders firmly and shout "Are you okay?"
3. Look at the chest for up to ten seconds. Gasping is not normal breathing.
4. If there is no response and no normal breathing, call emergency services now. Put the phone on speaker.
5. If someone else is with you, send them for an AED and to guide rescuers.
6. Lay the person on their back on a firm, flat surface.
7. Kneel beside the chest. Place the heel of one hand on the center of the chest, on the lower half of the breastbone.
8. Place your other hand on top and interlock your fingers.
9. Lock your elbows and position your shoulders directly over your hands.
10. Push hard and fast. Press down five to six centimeters, about two inches.
11. Compress at 100 to 120 per minute. Ask the app to start the CPR metronome at 110 beats per minute and push on every beat.
12. Let the chest rise fully between compressions. Do not lean on the chest.
13. If you are trained and willing, give two breaths after every 30 compressions. Tilt the head back, lift the chin, pinch the nose, and blow for about one second until the chest rises.
14. If you are not trained, do continuous compressions only. Do not stop for breaths.
15. If an AED arrives, turn it on and follow its voice prompts. Continue compressions while pads are placed.
16. Change rescuers every two minutes if someone else can help. Switch fast, in under five seconds.
17. Continue until the person breathes normally, moves, trained help takes over, or you cannot physically continue.
18. If the person starts breathing, roll them onto their side and keep watching them until help arrives.
