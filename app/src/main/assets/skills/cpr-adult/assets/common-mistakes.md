# Common CPR mistakes

1. Pushing too slowly. Match the metronome. Slower than 100 per minute moves too little blood.
2. Pushing too fast. Faster than 120 per minute does not let the heart refill.
3. Pushing too shallow. Aim for five to six centimeters. A strong push may crack ribs. Keep going anyway.
4. Leaning on the chest between pushes. Let it come all the way up every time.
5. Pausing too long. Any break longer than ten seconds hurts. Keep pauses short for breaths, AED checks, or rescuer switches.
6. Hands in the wrong place. Use the center of the chest, lower half of the breastbone, not the ribs or the stomach.
7. Bent elbows. Lock your arms and use your body weight, not your arm muscles.
8. Checking for a pulse repeatedly. Lay rescuers should not stop to check pulses. Watch for breathing or movement instead.
9. Waiting to start. Compressions started within seconds matter more than perfect technique.
10. Giving up early. Fatigue is normal after two minutes. Swap with someone else if you can, otherwise keep going.
11. Delaying the AED. If one is nearby, use it as soon as it arrives.
12. Skipping the call for help. Emergency services and directions to your location are as important as compressions.
