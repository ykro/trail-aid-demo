# Fracture or sprain steps

1. Stop the activity. Sit or lie the person down where they are.
2. If the person fell hard, hit their head, or has neck or back pain, do not move them. Call for help.
3. Look for bleeding. Treat serious bleeding first.
4. Look at the injured limb. Compare it with the other side. Note any deformity, swelling, or bruising.
5. Do not try to straighten or realign a deformed limb. Support it in the position you find it.
6. Remove rings, watches, and tight items before swelling makes it impossible. Leave a boot on if the ankle may be broken and you must walk out.
7. Check circulation below the injury. Press a fingernail or toenail. Color should return in two seconds. Feel for warmth. Ask if they can feel your touch.
8. Rest: keep the limb still and do not put weight on it.
9. Ice: wrap snow, cold water, or a cold pack in cloth and hold it on the injury. Ask the app to start a timer named "ice" for twenty minutes. Remove it when the timer ends. Never put ice directly on skin.
10. Compress: wrap an elastic bandage snugly from below the injury upward. Not so tight that it hurts or numbs.
11. Elevate: raise the limb above heart level when resting.
12. Give pain medication the person normally takes, if they are alert and not allergic.
13. Recheck circulation every fifteen minutes and after any bandage or splint. Loosen anything that turns fingers or toes cold, pale, or blue.
14. If the limb is deformed, the person cannot bear weight, or you must move over rough ground, tell the app you need a splint.
15. Evacuate or call for help if the bone is visible, the limb is deformed, the hip, thigh, or pelvis is injured, or the person cannot walk.
16. A mild sprain with normal circulation can often walk out slowly with support. Rest often and reassess.
17. Any injury with lasting pain, swelling, or difficulty bearing weight should be seen by a clinician.
