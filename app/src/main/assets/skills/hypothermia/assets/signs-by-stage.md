# Hypothermia signs by stage

Watch for the umbles: mumbles, fumbles, stumbles, and grumbles. They are early warning signs.

Mild hypothermia

1. Strong shivering the person cannot stop.
2. Cold hands and feet, pale skin, and goosebumps.
3. Mumbles: slurred or slow speech.
4. Fumbles: trouble with zippers, buckles, or laces.
5. Stumbles: clumsy walking.
6. Grumbles: irritable, withdrawn, or unusually quiet.
7. The person is still alert and answers questions correctly.
8. Action: shelter, dry layers, sugar, and movement can fix this in the field.

Moderate hypothermia

1. Shivering becomes weak or stops even though the person is still cold.
2. Confusion, poor judgment, or refusing help.
3. Cannot walk a straight line for ten steps.
4. Drowsy, slow, or apathetic.
5. May try to remove clothing because they feel warm.
6. Action: stop all activity, keep the person lying down, insulate fully, warm the core, and call for help.

Severe hypothermia

1. No shivering at all.
2. Unresponsive or barely responsive.
3. Very slow or hard to find breathing and pulse.
4. Stiff muscles and blue or gray skin.
5. May look dead. A cold person is not dead until they are warm and dead.
6. Action: handle extremely gently, keep flat, insulate, call emergency services, and check breathing for one minute before starting CPR.
