# Hypothermia steps

1. Stop moving further into cold. Find or make shelter now: behind rocks, under trees, or in a tent or bivy.
2. Get the person out of wind and rain.
3. Put insulation under the person: a sleeping pad, pack, rope, or pine boughs. The ground steals heat.
4. Remove wet clothing. Do this gently and one layer at a time, inside the shelter if possible.
5. Put on dry clothes, a hat, and gloves. Cover the neck.
6. Wrap the person in a sleeping bag, blankets, or an emergency blanket. Add a tarp or jacket outside as a wind and vapor barrier.
7. If the person is alert and can swallow, give warm sweet drinks and sugary food. Sugar fuels shivering.
8. Do not give alcohol.
9. Fill bottles with warm, not hot, water. Wrap them in cloth and place on the chest, armpits, and groin.
10. Do not rub or massage the arms and legs. Do not put the person in a hot bath.
11. Handle the person gently. Avoid sudden movements or rough carrying. A cold heart is fragile.
12. If the person is confused, clumsy, or not shivering, keep them lying flat and do not let them walk.
13. Ask the app to start a timer named "rewarm" for thirty minutes and recheck when it ends.
14. If shivering stops while the person is still cold, or they become drowsy or confused, call emergency services.
15. If the person becomes unresponsive, check breathing for up to one minute. Cold breathing is very slow.
16. If not breathing, start CPR and get help.
17. A mildly cold person who warms up, stops shivering, and is thinking clearly can continue with dry clothing and food.
18. Anyone who was moderate or severe should be evacuated and checked by a clinician, even if they seem better.
